The Culinary Heritage of Pune

Long before the new-wave cafés, these are the joints that shaped what Pune eats — bakeries, misal stalls, Irani cafes and thali houses, several dating back to the pre-independence era. New places keep opening, and plenty of them are great, but these are the originals. Not ranked — just listed.
